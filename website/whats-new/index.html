What's new in diri

The highlights of each release. Every change is in the release notes.

diri 0.10.0

October 18, 2026

Talk with your agent

When an agent finishes, diri reads a short summary aloud. Answer by voice and end with "send to agent".

Agents can use your Mac's apps

A background computer tool drives real Mac apps while your cursor, keyboard, and focus stay yours.

Model prices stay current

Claude and Codex cost estimates use rates fetched from LiteLLM when the release is built, including new model ids such as gpt-6.1-sol.

AGPL-3.0

diri stays free, including for commercial use. If you distribute a modified copy, or let people use one over a network, you must offer them its source under the same license.

diri 0.9.0

October 1, 2026

Notes

Think next to your agents. Type / for blocks and @ to link a session; every note is plain Markdown on disk.

To-dos that start agents

Press ⌃⌘↩ on a to-do to hand it to an agent with the note as context. Its progress shows up right under the to-do.

Search every note

⇧⌘F finds a note by anything it says, not just its title.
