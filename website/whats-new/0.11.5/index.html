diri 0.11.5: Mermaid diagrams in notes

diri 0.11.5 draws Mermaid diagrams in notes, lets you drag sessions between projects, keeps your selected row visible when you fold, and makes remote sessions sturdier.

Mermaid diagrams in notes

Write a ```mermaid block and the note draws the diagram.

Drag sessions anywhere

Drag a session and the rows make room as you go. Drop it on another project to move it there; the list scrolls when you drag near its edge.

Folding keeps your place

Folding a session or project no longer hides the row you're working in. It stays visible until you select something else, and ⌘J or Quick Open no longer unfolds things for good.

A better Links popover

Filter links by kind, read pull request states at a glance, and press Esc to close the popover without interrupting the agent.

Sturdier remote sessions

Remote agents survive large pastes, recoverable Holder errors, and a full disk or file-descriptor limit on the host.

And more

IME input composes at the cursor, notes open on your iPhone, a corrupt prefs.json no longer stops launch, and idle-hiding keeps sessions with a running background shell.

Every fix and smaller change is in the full release notes. diri updates itself; this release shows up as a restart prompt when it is ready.

Get diri 0.11.5Free and open source. macOS 15 or newer, Linux in beta. Bring the agent CLIs and accounts you already have.
