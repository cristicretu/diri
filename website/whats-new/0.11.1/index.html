diri 0.11.1: Hold ⌘T for anything new

diri 0.11.1 adds a launch ring on a held ⌘T, find in notes, smoother note scrolling, and safer computer use for agents working side by side.

Hold ⌘T for anything new

Hold ⌘T and a ring opens under the pointer: flick toward an agent, Terminal or Note and let go. A tap still starts your default agent, and holding the keys never starts more than one.

Talk with your agent

When an agent finishes, diri reads what matters aloud. Answer out loud and end with “send to agent”. Everything stays on this Mac.

Agents use your Mac, side by side

Agents open apps in the background, each with its own cursor, and ten can work at once without touching your mouse or keyboard. Computer use now waits for controls to be ready and checks each action landed.

Find in a note

⌘F searches the open note, every match highlighted. ⌘B makes text bold.

Smoother notes

Notes scroll smoothly, wide tables scroll sideways on their own, and the line you're reading holds still.

Every fix and smaller change is in the full release notes. diri updates itself; this release shows up as a restart prompt when it is ready.

Get diri 0.11.1Free and open source. macOS 15 or newer, Linux in beta. Bring the agent CLIs and accounts you already have.
